I am an undergraduate in pure mathematics and computer science at UNSW Sydney. 
My current academic interests are situated broadly within Category Theory, 
in particular studying higher category theory, topos theory and various applications 
in logic and formalization. My interests aren't strictly limited to those areas; I tend to follow
wherever my curiosity leads to. 

I use this site to maintain a blog which will share some notes or rough sketches of ideas that I'm recently studying or interested in, mathematical or not. You can browse through them using 
[[tags]]. 

Always open to feedback and discussion; whether you have a question about a 
post or simply want to chat about a shared interest, I would love to hear from you.
