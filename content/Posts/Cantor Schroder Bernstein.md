This is a short post which proves the intuitive yet tricky result, it fully follows the exercise of leinster which guides using a fixed point argument seen a lot. 

**Theorem (Cantor-Schroder-Bernstein).** Let $A$ and $B$ be sets. If $\lvert A \rvert \leq \lvert B \rvert \leq \lvert A \rvert$ then $A \cong B$.
